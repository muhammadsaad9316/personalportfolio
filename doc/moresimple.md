# Portfolio Experience

The portfolio should feel like an interactive visual experience.

The animations should be noticeable and impressive because the website will also be shared on social media. However, the animations should remain smooth, controlled, and easy to follow.

## Hero Section

The hero shows two sides of my work:

**Left side: Designer**  
**Right side: Developer**

When the visitor moves the mouse to the left, the Designer side becomes stronger:

- The lighting becomes softer.
- The light clothes appear.
- Design cards become clearer.
- Developer elements become quieter.

When the visitor moves the mouse to the right, the Developer side becomes stronger:

- The lighting becomes darker.
- The dark clothes appear.
- Code and terminal elements become clearer.
- Design elements become quieter.

When the mouse returns to the centre, both sides become balanced again.

The person, cards, and background should also move slightly with the mouse to create depth.

This transformation should be one of the main impressive moments of the website.

## Hero to Work Transition

When the visitor scrolls:

1. The navigation, headline, and floating cards fade away.
2. The person stays visible and slowly becomes larger.
3. The black wave rises from the bottom.
4. The screen becomes almost completely black.
5. The black layer moves away.
6. The cream Work section appears underneath.

This transition should feel cinematic and be strong enough to use as a short social-media clip.

## Work Section

The Work section begins with:

**Digital products.**  
**Real impact.**

The text appears first.

Then the centre circle appears, the connecting lines draw outward, and the four projects enter.

When the visitor hovers over a project:

- That project becomes larger and clearer.
- Its connecting line becomes active.
- The other projects become quieter.
- A “View Case Study” label follows the cursor.
- The centre graphic reacts toward the selected project.

The interaction should clearly show that every project is connected to a real problem and result.

## Work to Case Studies Transition

When the visitor finishes the Work section and continues scrolling:

1. The four project previews fade slightly.
2. The connecting lines dissolve.
3. The centre impact circle becomes smaller.
4. The heading **Digital products. Real impact.** moves upward and fades.
5. The section title appears:
   **CASE STUDIES**  
   **A closer look at the thinking behind the work.**
6. The first case study smoothly fills the screen.

## Shorter Case Studies

The Case Studies section should explain:

**The problem → The approach → The solution → The result**

The first and strongest project can have a more cinematic presentation.

Its main image stays visible while the visitor scrolls through a short story:

1. Project introduction
2. The problem
3. The approach
4. The solution
5. The result

The image can zoom or move slightly as each part appears.

This case study should feel impressive, but it should not require too much scrolling.

The remaining projects should be shorter. Each one can show:

- One large project visual
- A short problem
- The main solution
- The most important result
- A link to view the full project

This keeps the website exciting without making visitors scroll through four very long experiences.

## Case Study Ending

After the final project, show:

**Different problems.**  
**Same approach.**

**Understand deeply. Design clearly. Build properly.**

Then move into the final contact section.

## Final Contact Section

The final section should become quiet and simple.

Use:

**One last thing.**

**Good ideas deserve to be built properly.**

**If you have an idea or project, I’d like to hear about it.**

**Tell me about your project ↗**

Only use typography, spacing, and a small amount of movement here.

- Contact
- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Email
- Back to top

The footer should feel like a calm ending after the more animated parts of the website.

## Animation Approach

Use:

- **GSAP and ScrollTrigger** for the main cinematic animations
- **Lenis** for smooth scrolling
- **CSS and SVG** for smaller effects

The website should have a few unforgettable animation moments instead of making every element move.

The main moments are:

1. Designer and Developer hero transformation
2. Hero-to-Work black transition
3. Interactive Work project network
4. One strong cinematic case study

These moments should make the website memorable, demonstrate my development skills, and create impressive clips for social media.
